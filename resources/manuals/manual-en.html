Poultry Farm Manager — User Manual

Version 1.4 · For farm owners, managers and workers

1. What this program does

Poultry Farm Manager keeps the daily records of your farm on this computer:

  • live hens in every cage (deaths, removals, additions and transfers),
  • eggs collected (good, damaged and rejected) and egg production %,
  • feed, water and medicine used,
  • store stock (feed, medicines and other supplies),
  • start-of-day and end-of-day reports (PDF and Excel), weekly and monthly summaries.

It works without internet. All records are stored on this computer, so make regular backups (see section 11).

2. Users and passwords

There are two kinds of users:

User typeCan do
Administrator (manager / owner)Everything: settings, users, cages, corrections, closing days, amendments, restoring backups.
Data entry (worker)Start a day, enter eggs, feed, water, deaths and medicine, make backups.
  • Each person should have their own user name. Every change is recorded with the name of the person who made it.
  • To add a worker: Settings → Users → Add user.
  • To change your own password: Settings → Change my password.
  • If a worker forgets a password, an administrator can use Reset password. Keep the administrator password safe.

2.1 Creating your own account (sign up)

  1. On the sign-in screen click Create an account.
  2. Enter your full name, a user name and a password (twice), then click Send request.
  3. Your recovery code is shown (for example K7MQ-4XRT-9PWD). Write it down and keep it safe, then tick I have written down the code.
  4. Ask the administrator to approve you. Until then you cannot sign in.

The administrator sees waiting requests on the dashboard and in Settings → Users, chooses Data entry or Administrator, and clicks Approve (or Reject). Sign-up can be switched off in Settings → Farm.

2.2 Forgot your password?

  1. On the sign-in screen click Forgot password?.
  2. Enter your user name, your recovery code and a new password (twice), then click Reset password.
  3. A new recovery code is shown — the old one no longer works. Write the new one down.

Every user receives a recovery code when the account is created (the administrator's code is shown at the end of the first-time setup). If a code is lost, create a new one in Settings → Change my password → Create a new recovery code, or ask an administrator (the shield icon in Settings → Users). After 5 wrong passwords or codes the account is locked for 15 minutes.

3. Language

Click සිංහල or English at the bottom-left of the screen (or on the sign-in screen). The choice is remembered.

4. First-time setup

When the program is opened for the first time it asks for:

  1. Farm details — farm name, location, time zone (Sri Lanka: Asia/Colombo) and the first day you will record (usually today).
  2. Cages & hens — the name of each cage and the number of live hens in it now.
  3. Starting stock — feed and medicine in the store today. Medicines need a batch number and expiry date.
  4. Administrator — the manager's user name and password.
Try it first: on the first screen you can choose Explore with sample data. This opens a separate demonstration farm with a red "DEMONSTRATION DATA" bar. Demo records are kept in a different file and can never mix with your real records. Click Exit demo to leave it.

5. The daily routine

Every day follows the same steps:

  1. Morning — Start the day (Daily Operations).
  2. During the day — enter records: eggs (each collection), feed, water, deaths and treatments.
  3. Evening — review and close the day (Daily Operations, administrator).

5.1 Starting the day

Open Daily Operations. The date at the top is today. Click Start day.

  • The opening hen count of each cage is carried forward automatically from yesterday's closing count.
  • Store stock is also carried forward.
  • If the previous day was not closed, or days were missed, a yellow warning is shown. Read it, tick I understand and want to continue, then start.
  • An administrator can correct an opening count after a physical recount. A reason is required and is kept in the history.

A day can be: Not started, In progress, Ready to close or Closed.

5.2 Changing the date

Use the date box at the top-right of each screen. The arrows go to the previous and next day. Today returns to the current date.

6. Egg collection

Open Egg Collection.

  1. Choose the collection session (Morning, Midday, Evening or Extra).
  2. For each cage type Good eggs, Cracked / damaged and Other rejected.
  3. Click Save. Rows you changed are highlighted until saved.
  • Each cage can have one record per session, so the same collection can never be counted twice. To correct a number, change it and save again.
  • Collected = good + damaged + rejected. Saleable = good eggs.
  • Egg production % = eggs collected ÷ average live hens × 100. The average is (opening hens + closing hens) ÷ 2. An administrator can change this in Settings → Calculations.

7. Feed

Open Feed Management, choose the feed type, then for each cage enter what you know:

FieldMeaning
Feed at cage (start)Feed left in the feeder from yesterday (filled in automatically if counted yesterday).
Issued from storeFeed taken from the store to the cage. This is deducted from store stock once.
Returned unusedFeed brought back to the store (added back to stock).
Spilled / wastedFeed that fell or spoiled — it is not counted as eaten.
Feed at cage (end)Feed left in the feeder at the end of the day, if you count it.
Measured consumptionIf you weigh exactly what was eaten.

How consumption is worked out:

  1. Measured — if measured consumption is entered, it is used.
  2. Calculated from counts — if start and end feed at the cage are both counted: start + issued − returned − wasted − end.
  3. Estimate — otherwise: issued − returned − wasted. Estimates are marked with * and "Estimate". The program never assumes that all issued feed was eaten.

8. Water

Open Water Management. For each cage either:

  • enter the meter reading at start and end (litres used = end − start; the start reading is filled from yesterday's end reading), or
  • choose Entered manually and type the litres used.

If the end reading is lower than the start reading the program will not save it. If the meter was reset or replaced, an administrator clicks the spanner icon and records the reason and the litres used.

The program does not measure water by itself; it uses the numbers you enter.

9. Hen mortality and flock balance

Open Hen Mortality.

  • Record movement — deaths, removals (culls or sales, with a reason) or additions (new hens, with a reason).
  • Transfer between cages — moves hens from one cage to another. The farm total does not change.
  • The balance for each cage is: Closing = Opening + Added + Moved in − Moved out − Deaths − Removed. A cage can never go below zero.
  • Mortality % = deaths ÷ opening hens × 100.
  • At the end of the day, confirm for each cage that all deaths were recorded (Confirm deaths recorded), even if there were none.

10. Medicine and health

Open Medicine & Health.

  • Record treatment — choose the medicine and batch, the cage (or the whole farm), quantity, time, reason and who prescribed or approved it. The quantity is deducted from that batch.
  • Record health event — observations, vaccinations, veterinary visits, suspected illness. These do not change stock.
  • The Medicine batches tab shows stock and expiry dates. Expired and soon-to-expire batches are also shown on the dashboard.
The program does not prescribe medicines or suggest doses. Record treatments exactly as instructed by the farm manager or veterinarian.

11. Closing the day

In the evening an administrator opens Daily Operations and checks the End-of-day review: every cage with opening hens, deaths, closing hens, eggs, feed, water and medicine.

The closing checklist lists anything that needs attention:

  • Red items (for example, deaths not confirmed, feed figures that do not add up) must be fixed.
  • Yellow items (for example, a missing water record or unusually high mortality) can be fixed, or accepted with Accept with reason if there is a genuine reason. When several cages have the same problem, Accept all … with one reason saves time.
  • Blue items are information only.

When nothing is left, the status becomes Ready to close. Add manager comments if needed and click Close day. The program:

  1. saves a closing snapshot,
  2. saves the end-of-day PDF report in Documents\Poultry Farm Manager\Reports,
  3. updates the dashboard,
  4. records who closed the day and when,
  5. protects the day's records from changes.

Missing figures are never invented: they stay "Not recorded" in the report, and accepted items are listed with their reasons.

Leave in progress — if you cannot finish, simply leave the day open and continue tomorrow; nothing is lost.

11.1 Correcting a closed day (amendment)

If a mistake is found later, an administrator opens the day and clicks Amend closed day, enters the reason, makes the corrections and clicks Finish amendment. The program recalculates the following days' opening hen counts and keeps a full history of the amendment.

12. Dashboard

The dashboard shows today's figures, store stock, cages with missing records, and alerts (low stock, expiring medicines, high mortality, production drops, failed backups). Use the cage list and the 7 / 30 / 90-day buttons to change the charts. Gaps in charts mean the day was not recorded.

Alerts about production or mortality are information only; they are not a diagnosis of the cause.

13. Reports

Open Reports & Analytics.

  • Daily reports — Start-of-day (opening hens, store stock, pending tasks) and End-of-day (final figures) as PDF or Excel.
  • Weekly / monthly summary — choose This week, Last week, This month, Last month or any dates.
  • Cage comparison — compares cages over a period.
  • Audit history (administrator) — every change, who made it and why.

Reports are saved where you choose; recent reports can be opened again from the list.

14. Inventory

Open Inventory. Stock is never changed directly — every change is a movement that is kept forever: purchase, receipt, issue, usage, return, wastage, expired write-off, or (administrator) adjustment with a reason after a stock count.

Set a low-stock level for each item to get a warning before you run out. Feed issued in Feed Management and medicines used in treatments are deducted automatically.

15. Cage management

Administrators can rename cages, add new cages or make a cage inactive (it is then left out of new days; its history is kept). The starting number of hens cannot be changed after days have been recorded — use an opening correction in Daily Operations instead.

16. Backup and restore

Why

All records are on this computer. If the computer is damaged or stolen, only a backup can bring them back.

Automatic backups

By default a backup is made every 24 hours into Documents\Poultry Farm Manager\Backups, keeping the last 14. Change this in Backup & Restore → Automatic backups. If an automatic backup fails, a red warning appears on the dashboard.

Manual backup (recommended weekly, to a USB drive)

  1. Insert the USB drive.
  2. Open Backup & Restore and click Back up to a folder / USB drive….
  3. Choose the USB drive and click Select Folder.
  4. Wait for "Backup saved". Keep the USB drive in a safe place away from the computer.

Backup files are named like PoultryFarm-20261013-180000-manual.pfmbackup. Each backup is checked for damage before it is reported as successful.

Restoring a backup (administrator)

  1. Open Backup & Restore → Choose backup file… and select the .pfmbackup file.
  2. The program checks the file and shows the farm name and the last recorded day.
  3. Type RESTORE and click Replace current data with this backup.
  4. A safety backup of the current data is made first, then the backup is restored. Sign in again.

To move the farm to a new computer: install the program on the new computer, complete a quick setup or explore the demo, sign in as administrator and restore your latest backup.

18. Updating and uninstalling

  • Automatic updates: when the computer is connected to the internet, the program checks for a new version. An administrator sees a blue bar: Version … is available → Download update → Install and restart. A backup of the farm data is made first, then the program closes, updates and opens again. You can also check in Settings → Farm → Software updates, or switch automatic checks off there.
  • Without internet, run the new setup file you receive on a USB drive. Your records are kept.
  • Uninstalling removes the program but keeps your farm records in %APPDATA%\Poultry Farm Manager\data. Make a backup before uninstalling anyway.

19. Admin portal and duties (administrators)

Open Admin portal (menu → System).

  • Overview — active users, database size, last backup, database health, failed sign-ins, activity charts, and who looks after each cage.
  • Users & duties — approve or reject account requests, add users, reset passwords, issue recovery codes, and set duties: click the clipboard icon next to a user, tick the jobs they may do (start the day, eggs, feeding, water, deaths, medicine, store stock, receiving deliveries, egg sales, reports, backups) and choose the cages they look after.

- A user only sees the menu pages for their duties and can only enter or change records for their own cages. They can still view figures. - Each user's dashboard shows My duties today, ticked off automatically as the work is recorded.

  • Database — where the data file is, its size and version, how many records each part holds, Check database now (full integrity check) and Optimise.
  • Activity log — every change: who, when, what and why.

20. Feed & supplies received

Open Feed & supplies in. Click Receive a delivery, choose the supplier (or add a new one with +), enter the invoice and vehicle number, and add one line per item (quantity, unit cost; batch and expiry for medicines). Saving adds the stock to the store once and gives the delivery a number such as GRN-20261016-001.

The page shows feed received, purchase cost, feed in store and charts of feed received against feed issued to the cages. An administrator can cancel a delivery entered by mistake (the stock is removed again, unless it has already been used).

21. Egg store & sales

Every good and cracked egg collected goes into the egg store automatically; rejected eggs do not. Open Egg store & sales:

  • Send eggs — choose the customer, enter good and cracked eggs with their price per egg (trays of 30 are shown to help), the amount paid now and how, vehicle and driver. You cannot send more eggs than the store holds. Each dispatch gets a number such as DSP-20261016-001.
  • Record payment — for dispatches not fully paid. The Not fully paid tab lists what customers still owe.
  • Store corrections (administrator) — eggs broken in the store (−) or found in a count (+), with a reason.
  • Charts show eggs in and out, eggs in store, sales and money received.

22. Farm flow, quick search and the top bar

  • Farm flow shows the whole chain for 7, 30 or 90 days: feed received → feed in store → issued to cages → hens → eggs collected → eggs sent out → sales and money owed, with charts.
  • Ctrl + K (or the search box at the top) — type part of a page or action name, e.g. "eggs" or "dispatch", and press Enter.
  • New — quick actions: record eggs, a death, feed, a delivery or a dispatch.
  • The bell shows alerts (low stock, expiring medicine, unusual figures, account requests, failed backups).
  • Collapse menu (bottom left) makes the menu narrow to give pages more room.
